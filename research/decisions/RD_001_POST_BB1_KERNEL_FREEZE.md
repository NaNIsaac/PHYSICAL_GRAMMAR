# RD-001 — Keep v0.4 Kernel Frozen After Blind Benchmark I

## Context
Blind Benchmark I completed 60 frozen cases with provisional totals G0=0, G1=7, G2=49, G3=4, G4=0. Recurrent semantic pressure was observed around roles, conditions, references, history, and equivalence, but no case yet forced a new primitive.

## Options
1. Promote recurring analysis concepts (`Role`, `Condition`, `Reference`, etc.) immediately.
2. Revise the kernel to v0.6 based on positive benchmark results.
3. Keep v0.4 frozen and run an adversarial counterexample campaign.

## Decision
Choose option 3.

## Reason
The benchmark supports the current kernel but does not establish that its expressivity is natural rather than permissive. Premature promotion would confuse recurring usefulness with ontological necessity.

## Reopen when
- a repeated genuine G4 appears;
- multiple cases require E3 escape-hatch encodings;
- an adversarial family exposes a reusable missing mechanism;
- independent/human encoding repeatedly disagrees for the same structural reason.
