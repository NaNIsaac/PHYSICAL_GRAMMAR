# Research Questions

Questions are allowed to remain unresolved. A question is not a grammar commitment.

- **Q001** — Is role-sensitive grounding necessary as an explicit reusable mechanism?
- **Q002** — Are prerequisites/conditions/reference relations instances of a more generic semantic dependency?
- **Q003** — Can naming modifiers be predicted from distinctions preserved by the semantic construction?
- **Q004** — Is Rich Physical Dimension (RPD) useful beyond the development cases that motivated it?
- **Q005** — How should non-Entity arguments and indices (time, wavelength, mode index, etc.) be represented?
- **Q006** — How should held-fixed and constrained derivative semantics be represented?
- **Q007** — Is Site an Entity role/subtype or a distinct semantic sort?
- **Q008** — How should geometric, probabilistic, and manifold-valued spaces be typed?
