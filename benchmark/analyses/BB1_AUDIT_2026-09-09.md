# BB1 audit — 2026-09-09

Status: evidence-availability and consistency audit, not independent regrading.
The original manifest, grades and kernel have not been changed.

## Findings

1. The repository contains a frozen 60-item manifest, a 60-row result ledger,
   an aggregate observation ledger and a blank case template. It does not contain
   the 60 individual external definitions, citations, semantic parses or knowledge
   accounts required by `docs/07_BLIND_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md`. Consequently the
   reported 60/60 completion is inherited evidence, not a reproducible audit result.
2. B018 is **specific heat capacity at constant pressure** in the manifest but
   **heat capacity at constant pressure** in the result ledger. These are distinct
   targets: the specific quantity includes mass normalization. The IUPAC entry
   [specific heat capacity](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/S05800/pdf)
   identifies that distinction. Restore the original case parse before deciding
   whether this is a label omission or an actual change of benchmark target.
3. G0/G1/G2 boundaries cannot be reproduced without a versioned list of the seed
   Properties and mathematical constructors available before BB1. The specification
   gives examples, but not an exhaustive benchmark environment. The seven G1 rows
   B021/22/23/38/51/52/57 are the first queue for reconstruction, not approved grades.
4. B014 and B015 deserve a definition-versus-model review in addition to G1/G2
   review. IUPAC gives definitions for
   [fugacity](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/F02543) and
   [activity coefficient](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/A00116/PDF).
   A conventional reference or limiting normalization alone does not demonstrate
   a constitutive-model commitment. The original parsed relation is needed to
   determine whether these G3 classifications are justified. No regrade is asserted.

## Disposition

- Keep G0=0, G1=7, G2=49, G3=4, G4=0 as **reported, provisional** totals.
- Do not describe this audit as a new blind run or an independent replication.
- Recovered historical records may repair provenance. Newly written parses must
  instead be dated and labeled retrospective reconstructions.
- No kernel revision follows from missing evidence or the B018 mismatch.
- Next audit unit: reconstruct B018 against its original target, then the seven
  G1 rows and B014/B015 with explicit seed/math/model accounting.
