# Blind Benchmark I — Observation Ledger

These are observations, not automatic grammar revisions.

## O001 Relational grounding
Quantity identity can depend on ordered or role-conditioned participation, not merely the set of Entities present.

## O002 Construction genealogy
Same grounding does not imply same Quantity. Moment of inertia/radius of gyration, phase/group velocity, and purity/von Neumann entropy are recurring minimal pairs.

## O003 Role-sensitive semantics
Source/target, before/after, driver/response, reactant/product, measurement target/indication recur. Their importance is established; a dedicated `Role` AST node is not.

## O004 Condition-bearing construction
Held-fixed conditions in derivatives are semantic content. Isothermal vs other constrained derivatives cannot be collapsed by deleting the subscript condition.

## O005 Binding generality
The same free/derived/bound/eliminated/context dependency discipline works for Site, Species, State, Scatterer and related domains.

## O006 Operator-dependent grounding transformation
Local differential operators typically preserve local grounding; integration/summation can bind the integration variable while preserving the domain/container/path grounding.

## O007 Coarse dimension is insufficient for Property identity
Examples: kerma/absorbed dose/equivalent dose share energy-per-mass dimension; stopping power/LET share energy-per-length; area/cross section share area dimension.

## O008 Transfer genealogy
Energy lost BY a projectile, imparted TO matter, transferred TO secondary charged particles, and locally transferred TO material are distinct constructions even when dimensions coincide.

## O009 Representation is not Projection
Invertible coordinate/representation changes (e.g. spectral density under frequency↔wavelength with Jacobian) differ from operations that discard distinctions (angle integration, magnitude-square phase loss).

## O010 Reference/convention dependence
Reference state, standard state, ideal behavior, vacuum comparison and width conventions recur, but are heterogeneous. Do not prematurely collapse them into one primitive.

## O011 Physical Expression is broader than named scalar Property
Calibration functions, constitutive relations, dispersion relations, fields, tensors and functionals validate the broader expression category.

## O012 Contextual role can reclassify an existing Property
A Property such as `Length[Rod]` becomes a measurand when targeted by a measurement context; contextual role and Property identity should not be conflated.

## Candidate findings (not yet promoted)
- **F-C1:** Quantity identity requires referential genealogy + construction genealogy + preserved distinctions, not grounding alone.
- **F-C2:** Grounding Closure generalizes across mathematical binding in diverse domains.
- **F-C3:** Many important semantic roles/conditions/references need preservation but are not yet justified as grammar primitives.
- **F-C4:** Coarse physical dimension can be interpreted as a semantics-losing projection.

## Required next test
Actively search for cases where the above claims fail, especially where existing categories only succeed through unnatural reification or annotation.
